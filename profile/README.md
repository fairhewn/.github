# Fairhewn

Websites, Google and AI search, ads and social media for interior design, fit-out and joinery firms in the UAE, the Gulf, Europe and the US.

Fairhewn is a one-person studio. Every site is designed and built by hand, in English and Arabic (the Arabic written as Arabic and laid out right to left), from the firm's own project photos, with WhatsApp on every page and plain facts that Google and AI assistants can read.
